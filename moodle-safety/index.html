Fundamentals of Laboratory Safety Moodle Course Development Project Project Overview Fundamentals of Laboratory Safety is a self-paced onboarding course designed for new employees working in medical laboratory environments. The course introduces essential laboratory safety practices and prepares learners to apply safe workplace behaviors before beginning hands-on work. This project demonstrates instructional design, learning experience design, assessment design, visual design, and Moodle course development skills. Course Design The course is organized into four learning modules, with instructional pages, visual elements, and knowledge checks that reinforce learner understanding. A final assessment requires learners to achieve a 100% score before the certificate of completion is released.
