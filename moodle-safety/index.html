Fundamentals of Laboratory Safety

Moodle Course Development Project

Project Overview

Fundamentals of Laboratory Safety is a self-paced onboarding course designed for new employees working in medical laboratory environments.

The purpose of this course is to provide new employees with essential knowledge and practices needed to work safely in a laboratory setting.

This Moodle course development project demonstrates instructional design, learning experience design, assessment design, visual design, and familiarity with Moodle as a learning platform.
