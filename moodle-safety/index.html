Course Design

Fundamentals of Laboratory Safety was designed as a self-paced onboarding course for new employees working in medical laboratory environments.

The course is organized into four learning modules that guide learners through essential laboratory safety concepts and workplace practices.

Each module includes instructional pages, visual elements, and knowledge checks to reinforce understanding before learners progress to the final assessment.

Learners must successfully complete the final assessment with a 100% score before receiving a certificate of completion.
