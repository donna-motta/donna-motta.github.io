Instructional Design Portfolio

I am an instructional designer with a 35-year background in multimedia journalism and higher education, along with 3 years of ESL teaching experience. I specialize in designing clear, structured, and engaging learning experiences for adult learners across academic and professional contexts.

I am advanced in Articulate Rise 360 and familiar with Articulate Storyline 360, with hands-on experience developing multiple eLearning projects in Rise 360. My work focuses on translating complex information into accessible, well-organized learning experiences that support learner understanding and performance.

My instructional design approach emphasizes clarity, structure, and learner engagement grounded in real-world communication and education experience.
