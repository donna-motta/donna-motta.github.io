⭐ High-Impact Feedback for Managers (SBI Framework)

Managers often struggle to deliver feedback that is clear, specific, and behavior-focused. This eLearning module, designed in Articulate Rise 360, introduces the SBI (Situation–Behavior–Impact) framework to improve the quality of performance conversations.

Learners apply the SBI model to transform general feedback into structured, behavior-based communication that increases clarity, accountability, and team performance.

Designed for leadership development and workplace communication training where consistent, actionable feedback strengthens team effectiveness.

Instructional approach: scenario-based practice focused on real-world communication skills.
