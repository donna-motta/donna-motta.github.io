⭐ High-Impact Feedback for Managers (SBI Framework)

Managers often struggle to deliver feedback that is clear, specific, and behavior-focused. This eLearning module, designed in Articulate Rise 360, introduces the SBI (Situation–Behavior–Impact) framework to support more effective performance conversations.

Learners practice turning general feedback into structured, behavior-based communication that improves clarity, accountability, and performance.

Designed for leadership development and workplace communication training where consistent, actionable feedback improves team effectiveness.

Instructional approach: scenario-based learning and applied communication practice.
