Instructional Design Projects

This portfolio showcases workplace learning solutions designed to improve performance, communication, and decision-making in real business environments.

Projects are built using Articulate Rise 360 and Storyline 360, with a focus on scenario-based learning and real-world application.

Projects

⭐ High-Impact Feedback for Managers (SBI Framework)

Managers often struggle to deliver feedback that is clear, specific, and behavior-focused. This eLearning module, designed in Articulate Rise 360, introduces the SBI (Situation–Behavior–Impact) framework to support more effective performance conversations.

Through realistic workplace scenarios, learners practice transforming general feedback into structured, behavior-based communication that improves clarity, accountability, and employee performance.

Designed for leadership development and workplace communication training where consistent, actionable feedback is essential to team performance.

Instructional approach: scenario-based learning, leadership development, and applied communication practice.

AI + Human Decision-Making in Hiring

Scenario-based learning simulation designed in Articulate Storyline 360 that helps learners evaluate when to rely on AI recommendations and when to apply human judgment in hiring decisions.

This module develops decision-making skills in HR and talent evaluation by placing learners in realistic hiring scenarios where they must balance automation tools with human oversight.

Designed for workplace learning environments where AI-supported decision-making is becoming standard practice.

Instructional approach: scenario-based learning, decision-making practice, and applied judgment training.

Business Writing: From Complexity to Clarity

Scenario-based eLearning course designed in Articulate Rise 360 that helps learners transform complex business communication into clear, concise, and actionable messages.

Through workplace scenarios, learners practice rewriting dense or unclear communication into structured, audience-focused messaging that improves understanding and decision-making.

Designed for workplace communication training where clarity, brevity, and action-oriented writing are essential for effective collaboration.

Instructional approach: scenario-based learning, applied business communication practice, and writing skill development.
