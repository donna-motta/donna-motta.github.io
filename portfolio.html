AI + Human Decision-Making in Hiring (Storyline Simulation)

Business Problem

Organizations are increasingly using AI to support hiring decisions, but risk over-reliance on algorithmic recommendations without sufficient human judgment and accountability.

Solution

I designed a scenario-based learning simulation that helps learners evaluate when to trust AI recommendations and when to apply human judgment in hiring decisions.

Instructional Design Approach

The experience uses branching decision paths where learners analyze AI-generated candidate recommendations and decide how to proceed with interviews and selection.

The design emphasizes human-AI collaboration, critical thinking, and responsible decision-making in modern HR workflows.

Outcome Focus

The simulation strengthens learners’ ability to interpret AI outputs critically while maintaining accountability for final hiring decisions.

Skills

Articulate Storyline 360, scenario-based learning, branching logic design, instructional design, AI-assisted decision-making design
